# ExamHub — Platform Knowledge Base

This is the ONLY trusted source for questions about how ExamHub works. If a question is not
answered here, say that you are not certain and suggest contacting the examiner or an
administrator. Do not guess at platform behaviour, and do not describe internal architecture,
configuration, or infrastructure — you do not have access to any of it.

## Accounts and roles

- Anyone can register from the **Get Started / Register** page. New accounts are **Candidates**.
- There are three roles: **Candidate** (takes exams), **Examiner** (writes questions, builds and
  publishes exams, releases results), and **Admin** (manages users, categories and examiner
  onboarding).
- To become an Examiner, use **Become Examiner / Apply as Examiner** and submit a request with
  your purpose. An Admin reviews it and approves or rejects it; you are notified of the outcome.
  Examiner access is not granted automatically.
- A deactivated account cannot sign in. An Admin controls activation.

## Taking an exam

- **Browse Exams** lists the exams currently open to you. An exam appears when it has been
  published by its examiner and is inside its scheduled window.
- **One attempt per exam.** Once you submit an exam, it is finished — you cannot retake it, and
  it no longer appears as available. Start only when you are ready.
- Answers are **saved automatically** as you go, so a refresh or a brief disconnection does not
  lose your work. You can resume an in-progress attempt.
- The timer is controlled by the **server**, not your browser. Closing the tab does not pause it.
  When time runs out the attempt is **auto-submitted** with whatever you have answered.
- **Leaving the exam tab is recorded.** Switching tabs or windows during an attempt increments a
  tab-switch count that the examiner can see. It is an integrity signal, not an automatic
  penalty, but it is best avoided.
- If a question looks wrong or broken, you can **report an issue** with it; the examiner reviews
  reported questions.

## Results

- **Your score and pass/fail status appear immediately after you submit** — scoring is
  automatic, so the result page opens right away with your marks.
- **The question-by-question review is not immediate.** Which answers were correct (and the
  topic performance chart) unlock only after the examiner **publishes the results** — this
  protects exam integrity while other candidates may still be taking the same exam. It is
  normal, not an error, and no one is being singled out.
- When results are published you receive a **notification**, the detailed review and chart
  unlock, and any earned certificate is issued.
- Pass or fail is decided by the exam's passing marks, set by the examiner.
- Once you complete an exam it **no longer appears in Browse Exams** — one attempt per exam.

## Analytics and the performance chart

- After you submit, ExamHub analyses your answers in the background and produces a **topic
  breakdown**: your percentage per topic (for example "LINQ Operations 80%").
- This runs shortly after submission, so a breakdown may be **briefly unavailable / still
  processing** right after you finish. Waiting a short while and refreshing usually resolves it.
- **Analytics** shows your total attempts, average score, pass rate, certificates earned, and
  your best score per exam. Opening an exam's chart shows the topic-by-topic radar view.
- Topic breakdowns only exist for exams whose results have been published.

## Certificates

- A certificate is issued when you **pass** an exam and its results have been published.
- **Certificates** lists everything you have earned, each with a certificate code, and can be
  downloaded.
- Failing an exam does not produce a certificate. A revoked certificate no longer counts.

## Notifications

- The bell in the navigation bar shows unread notifications — most commonly that results have
  been published, or that an examiner request has been decided.

## What the AI Learning Coach can and cannot do

- It can explain **your own** published results, identify your strong and weak topics, and build
  a study plan from them.
- It **cannot** show another person's results, reveal correct answers or question-bank content,
  release results early, or change any score, grade or certificate.
- Personalized coaching is available to **Candidates**, because it is built on candidate
  performance data. Examiners and Admins can use the coach for general platform guidance.
